✦ FAQ

Questions, answered

Can't find what you need? Talk to us.

Do I need to know how to code?

No. Describe what you want in plain English and Aiflow builds the agent. Developers can extend anything with our SDK.

Which AI models do you use?

Aiflow is model-agnostic. Use the latest frontier models or bring your own API keys.

Is my data secure?

Yes. Data is encrypted in transit and at rest, never used to train models, and enterprise plans include SOC 2 and data residency.

Can you build a custom agent for my business?

Absolutely — our solutions team designs, builds and maintains custom AI agents and automations for clients.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes, plans are month-to-month and you can cancel from your dashboard in one click.
